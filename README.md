# PROJECT NEZUMI — ONE MOON, TWO HEARTS 🌙🌸⚡
### Complete Interactive Karwa Chauth Website

A romantic, mobile-first interactive experience crafted as a Karwa Chauth surprise for a long-distance girlfriend. 

The boyfriend voluntarily kept a fast for her across the distance, even though she could not keep one herself. The website celebrates this gesture and their bond with reassurance, tenderness, and an anime-inspired festive aesthetic (*Nezuko & Zenitsu couple dynamic*).

---

## 📁 Exact Folder Structure

```text
moon/
│
├── index.html            # The complete interactive experience (HTML + CSS + SVG + Vanilla JS)
├── song.mp3              # (Optional) Drop your song / voice note here (or voice-note.mp3)
├── verify_site.js        # Automated test script verifying scenes, IDs, and customized text phrases
└── README.md             # Complete documentation and deployment guide
```

> **Zero External Dependencies:** Built with pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, SVG artwork, and JavaScript. No npm install, no build step, no paid APIs, and no framework required. Simply open `index.html` in any browser.

## 🎭 The 6 Interactive Scenes

1. **Scene 1 — The Moonlit Entrance**
   - Midnight violet sky with twinkling stars and drifting pink sakura petals.
   - A glowing full moon rises smoothly onto the screen.
   - Staged text reveal: *“A little surprise for my favourite person…”* → *“ONE MOON, TWO HEARTS.”*
   - Button: *“Come see the moon, meri jaan ✨”*

2. **Scene 2 — The Virtual Channi Ritual**
   - An ornate golden Indian sieve (*channi*) with brass mesh and hanging bells positioned in front of the moon.
   - A softly flickering earthen *diya* (clay lamp) with glowing flame.
   - Touch/click or press Space/Enter to lift the channi: it glides upward with a slight tilt, uncovering the moon which bursts in a radiant golden halo with a burst of sakura petals.
   - Text reveal: *“Chaand toh aaj bhi wahi hai… bas meri dua ka sabse pyaara hissa mujhse door hai. ❤️”*
   - Button: *“A little something from my heart”*

3. **Scene 3 — From My Heart to Yours ❤️🌙 (Meri Pyaari Jaan… 🥹🎀)**
   - Intimate letter on warm ivory parchment with gold corner filigree and candlelight glow.
   - Paragraph-by-paragraph staged fade-in with your personal words:
     - *Baby, pata hai, aaj maine aapke liye vrat rakha hu 🥺❤️*
     - *Karwa Chauth par wives apne husbands ki lambi umar ke liye vrat rakhti hain, unke liye preykarti hain… lekin yaar jab main apni poori zindagi aapke saath bitaana chahta hoon, toh prey karne ka haq sirf apke pass kyu rahe apun bhi kar skata hai na meri jaan samjhe 🫂❤️*
     - *Main bhi toh aapse utna hi pyaar karta hoon, meri jaan. Aapki khushi, aapki sehat, aapki khushi… mere liye bhi utni hi impppp hai Toh socha, aaj ka din main bhi aapke naam fast rakhiu. 🌙💗*
     - *Aap mere liye vrat nahi rakh paayi, and that's completely okay, baby🥹 Aap hain, aapka pyaar hai… mere liye wohi sabse badi baat hai🫶🏻❤️*
     - *Bas aaj chaand ko dekhte hue ek chhota sa sapna dekha… ki ek din hum dono saath honge. Koi distance nahi, koi phone screen nahi, koi video call par ek doosre ko miss karna nahi. Bas aap mere saamne hongi, main aapka haath pakde rahunga, aur dil mein yahi sukoon hoga ki yesss meri jaan we made it and we are together always 🥹🫂*
     - *Happy Karwa Chauth, meri pyaari jaan. Aap hamesha khush raho, healthy raho, haste raho… aur jab bhi zindagi mushkil lage, yaad rakhna ki kahin door ek insaan hai jo aapki smile karne ko 24 into 7 baitha hai . 🥹🎀*
     - *I love you more than these words can ever explain, meri jaan. Aaj bhi, kal bhi, aur un saare dino mein bhi jo hum abhi saath jeena baaki hain🫶🏻❤️🌸*
   - Button: *“Listen to my song 🎵🎧”*

4. **Scene 4 — Music / New Song Added In App Nezumi 🎧🌸⚡**
   - **Exclusive Feature Badge:** `✨ New song added in app Nezumi 🌸⚡`
   - Heading: *“Close your eyes, meri jaan. 🎧”*
   - Subtitle: *“I wish I could sing this while sitting right beside you. Until then, here's a little melody from my heart to yours.”*
   - Dedicated audio card with spinning glowing moon medallion, custom progress scrubber, time counter, and 21-bar pink & gold animated waveform visualizer.
   - Automatically loads `song.mp3` or `voice-note.mp3`. If not yet provided, clicking Play triggers a sweet celestial synthesized demo melody so she can test the player right away.
   - Button: *“Kaash aap yahin hoti… 💧🥹”*

5. **Scene 5 — Kaash Aap Yahin Hoti… 🥹❤️ (Paani Pilao Animation)**
   - Initial appearance with glowing moon, sakura petals, and the pulsing golden-pink button:
     **“Meko paani pilao, meri jaan 💧”**
   - **Dedicated 4-Phase Animated Sequence:**
     - **Phase 1 (She appears):** Golden bloom; woman in festive pink/red attire with delicate jewelry facing her beloved; man in festive sherwani facing her.
     - **Phase 2 (She offers water):** Woman gently extends both hands forward, cupping them together. A clear, sparkling pool of water appears nestled in her palms with ripples and glowing droplets. Man gently leans toward her hands.
     - **Phase 3 (He drinks):** Man's head and upper body smoothly bow down over her cupped palms. He gently drinks the water directly from her hands. The water level recedes with subtle head bobbing and ripples.
     - **Phase 4 (The emotional moment):** Man raises his head and smiles warmly at her; woman smiles back; a golden heart aura blooms between them, sakura petals float around, and the moon glows brighter.
   - Text reveal after the animation:
     - *Meri jaan, Karwa Chauth ki woh rasam dekho toh kitni pyaari lagti hai na? Wife apne pati ke liye vrat rakhti hai, chaand ko dekhti hai, aur phir pati apne haathon se use paani pilata hai… 🥹🫂❤️*
     - *Aur aaj maine socha… agar main aapke liye vrat rakh sakta hoon, toh apne dil mein ek chhoti si wish kyun na rakhun? Ki ek din aisa aaye jab aap mere saamne hon, chaand hum dono ne saath dekha ho, aur phir aap apne nanhe-nanhe haathon mein paani lekar mujhe pilao. 🥺💗*
     - *Main thoda sa aapki taraf jhukoon, aap pyaar se mujhe paani pilao, aur main paani peene ke baad bas aapko dekhta rahoon… aur smile kar doon. Shayad aap poochho, Kya hua, aise kyun dekh rahe ho?”🤭❤️*
     - *Aaj toh ye sab sirf imagination mein hai, aur is chhoti si website mein humara virtual moment… lekin ek din, meri jaan, main chahta hoon ki ye sirf animation na rahe. Main chahta hoon ki hum sach mein saath hon, aur ye chhoti si wish humari real memory ban jaaye. 🥹❤️*
     - *Tab tak, is virtual moment ko humare us din ke liye sambhaal ke rakhte hain… jab main screen ke us paar nahi, balki aapke bilkul saamne hounga🌙🫂💗*
   - Replay button: *“Can we do this for real one day? 🥹”* (smoothly replays the complete sequence).
   - Secondary button: *“Continue our little story 🌸”* (advances to Scene 6).

6. **Scene 6 — 🌸 PROJECT NEZUMI ⚡ (Happy Karwa Chauth, Meri Jaan. 🌙❤️)**
   - Silhouette of the couple resting together beneath the giant moon.
   - Closing love letter:
     - *No distance between us. No phone screen to hold. No goodnight whispered through a call before we both fall asleep missing each other.*
     - *Bas aap aur main… aapka haath mere haath mein, aapki smile mere saamne, aur dil mein woh sukoon jo shayad sirf aapke paas hone se milta hai. 🫂💗*
     - *Aaj meri preyyaapke liye thi. Kal bhi hogi. Aur ek din, jab hum saath honge, toh shayad main aapko ye sab dobara bataunga… bina kisi screen ke, bas aapka haath pakad kar 🌙🫶🏻*
     - *One moon. Two hearts. Different places, one little universe. ❤️*
     - *nezuko ke liye mai apke zenitsuuuu 🌸⚡❤️*
   - Reset button: *“Experience Our Little Universe Again ↺”* (smoothly resets the entire journey back to Scene 1).

## 🎵 How to Add Your Song:
- Name your audio file **`song.mp3`** (or **`voice-note.mp3`**).
- Place it in the `moon` folder alongside [`index.html`](file:///c:/all%20projects/moon/index.html).
- The player in Scene 4 will load it automatically!
